How a title gets made

A01 / 04

Math

Models written from scratch and replayed in full. RTP, hit rate and volatility are checked on every published round before a game leaves the studio.

A02 / 04

Art

One style per game, held from the first sketch to the lobby tile. No stock packs and no borrowed looks.

A03 / 04

Motion

Animation carries the mechanic. A win has to read at a glance, on a phone, with the sound off.

A04 / 04

Build

Light front ends that load fast and hold their frame rate, from a popout window to a phone in portrait.

Four aces. No title leaves the studio without all four.
