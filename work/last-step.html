Plant your flag on tile 2 to 6, then cross the temple bridge. A tile that gives way before the flag pays nothing; from the flag on, the farthest tile reached is the win. Reach the idol on tile 12 and the Vault opens.

7 modes with a top win of 10,000x. The math returns 96.30% on every mode and was replayed in full over 1,544,378 rounds.

  • Five flag positions, steady to wild
  • The Vault: a golden bridge with ropes
  • Vault and Grand Vault bonuses
