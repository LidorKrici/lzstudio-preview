The game

Choose a range and fire one bullet. It leaves the barrel worth nothing, collects a prize from every object it hits, and pays when it reaches the target. A bullseye pays double.

4 modes with a top win of 10,000x. The math returns 96.30% on every mode and was replayed in full over 610,238 rounds.
