How to play

  1. Choose your play amount and press PLAY (or the space bar).
  2. Your post goes live and its tiles flip one at a time. Each reaction adds to the post's multiplier.
  3. After every 5th reaction a Hype Check fires on its own. It always secures (locks in) the current multiplier and adds one effect.
  4. If a CANCELLED tile flips, the post ends and you win your secured multiplier × the play amount. If nothing is secured yet, the round ends with no win.
  5. If all 36 tiles flip without a cancel, the post Goes Viral: the multiplier doubles and you win it in full.
  6. Reactions and Hype Checks fill the round's Hype Meter. When it reaches 100%, the post goes live and the LIVE STREAM bonus starts (see below).

Win = play amount × final multiplier. Wins are capped at 5,000× the play amount: any round whose result would be higher wins exactly 5,000×. A Shield blocks one CANCELLED tile; a blocked tile stays on the board and the post keeps going.

Hype Meter

The Hype Meter starts empty at the start of every round and fills as the round plays. Each reaction and each Hype Check adds to it:

EventAdds to the meter
Heart / Fire / Rocket / Crown+1% / +2% / +4% / +8%
Hype Check: Lock / Shield+2% / +4%
Hype Check: Boost / Repost+12% / +35%

When the meter reaches 100% and at least 7 tiles are still closed, the post goes LIVE straight away. If a CANCELLED tile ends the round first, the meter's progress is lost; it never carries over to the next round. The log next to the meter lists what filled it this round.

LIVE STREAM bonus

Going live adds +1.00× to the post (a LIVE tile marks the moment) and starts the stream. The next 6 tiles are live tiles: each one multiplies the post's multiplier, and no CANCELLED can appear during the stream. When the 6th live tile flips, the stream ends and you win the full multiplier × the play amount.

Heart×1.1 or ×1.2
Fire×1.5
Rocket×2 or ×3
Crown×5

GO LIVE mode

The GO LIVE button starts a round straight in the LIVE STREAM bonus, from 10.00×, for 64× the play amount. You confirm the price before the round starts. The stream works exactly as above: 6 live tiles, then you win the full multiplier × the play amount. RTP in this mode is 96.30% and the max win is 5,000× the play amount.

There are no choices during a round. The whole round is decided by the game server when you press PLAY; the animation shows that result.

Controls

  • The big round PLAY button starts a round with the selected play amount. The space bar does the same. While a round plays, the button spins and waits.
  • − / + and the amount itself change the play amount. MIN and MAX jump to the smallest and largest amounts.
  • TURBO (the lightning button left of PLAY) speeds up the animations.
  • AUTO (the button right of PLAY) opens the autoplay panel. Choose how many plays, and optionally stop when a single win reaches a size you pick, when your balance falls by a set amount, or when a LIVE STREAM starts. Nothing starts until you press Start autoplay. Autoplay always uses the BASE mode at the selected play amount. Press STOP (or the space bar) at any time; the current round finishes first. Autoplay also stops if your balance is too low for the next play.
  • GO LIVE opens a confirmation showing the price (64× the play amount). Confirm to start a round straight in the LIVE STREAM bonus.
  • The speaker button turns sound and music on or off. The ⓘ button opens these rules.
  • Win shows what the round has secured so far, and the final win at the end.
  • The Hype Meter shows this round's progress toward the LIVE STREAM, with a log of what filled it. The streak label above it counts rounds in a row that won at least the play amount; it is a label only.
