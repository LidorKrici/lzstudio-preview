Going Viral · Game rules

Modes

ModePriceRTPMax win

Board: 36 tiles (6 × 6). Every mode has an RTP of 96.30%. The expected return is calculated over many plays.

How to play

  1. Pick a risk: CHILL, TRENDING or VIRAL (see below). Choose your play amount and press PLAY (or the space bar).
  2. Your post goes up and its tiles flip one at a time. Each reaction adds to the post's multiplier.
  3. After every few reactions (3 in CHILL, 5 in TRENDING, 7 in VIRAL) a Hype Check fires on its own. It always secures (locks in) the current multiplier and adds one effect.
  4. If a CANCELLED tile flips, the post ends and you win your secured multiplier × the play amount. If nothing is secured yet, the round ends with no win.
  5. If all 36 tiles flip without a cancel, the post Goes Viral: the multiplier doubles and you win it in full.
  6. Reactions and Hype Checks fill the round's Hype Meter. When it is full, the post goes live and the LIVE STREAM bonus starts (see below).

Win = play amount × final multiplier. Wins are capped at the mode's max win: any round whose result would be higher wins exactly the max win. A Shield blocks one CANCELLED tile; a blocked tile stays on the board and the post keeps going.

Hype Meter

The Hype Meter starts empty at the start of every round and fills as the round plays. Each reaction and each Hype Check adds points to it. It is full at points.

Heart / Fire / Rocket / Crown1 / 2 / 4 / 8

When the meter is full and at least 7 tiles are still closed, the post goes LIVE straight away. If a CANCELLED tile ends the round first, the meter's progress is lost; it never carries over to the next round.

LIVE STREAM bonus

Going live adds +1.00× to the post (a LIVE tile marks the moment) and starts the stream. The next 6 tiles are live tiles: each one multiplies the post's multiplier, and no CANCELLED can appear during the stream. When the 6th live tile flips, the stream ends and you win the full multiplier × the play amount.

Instant bonus

GO LIVE starts a round straight in the LIVE STREAM from , for × the play amount. Its live tiles are the CHILL and TRENDING ones.

SUPER LIVE starts a round straight in the LIVE STREAM from , for × the play amount. Its live tiles are hotter (see the table above); there are no ×1.1 tiles.

Both work exactly like the stream above: 6 live tiles, then you win the full multiplier × the play amount. You confirm the price before the round starts. RTP in both is 96.30% and the max win is 5,000× the play amount.

There are no choices during a round. The whole round is decided by the game server when you press PLAY; the animation shows that result.

Controls

  • The big round PLAY button (at the right of the bar) starts a round with the selected play amount and risk mode. The space bar does the same.
  • While a round plays, PLAY shows a square: press it (or the space bar) to skip, and the rest of the round plays at high speed. The result is already decided, so skipping never changes what you win.
  • − / + beside the amount, or the amount itself, change the play amount. They rest while a round plays.
  • Balance shows your balance. Win shows what the round has secured so far, and the final win at the end. When the next round starts it keeps showing that win for a moment, labelled Last win.
  • CHILL / TRENDING / VIRAL, beside the board, choose the risk mode for the next rounds. They cannot be changed while a round plays.
  • TURBO (the lightning button left of PLAY) speeds up the animations. Press it again to turn it off.
  • AUTO (the button right of PLAY) opens the autoplay panel. Choose how many plays, and optionally stop when a single win reaches a size you pick, when your balance falls by a set amount, or when a LIVE STREAM starts. Nothing starts until you press Start autoplay. Autoplay uses the selected risk mode and play amount, and AUTO counts the plays left. Press PLAY (or the space bar) at any time to stop; the current round finishes first. Autoplay also stops if your balance is too low for the next play.
  • The GO LIVE sign beside the board opens the bonus panel: pick GO LIVE or SUPER LIVE; each shows its price. The panel also has the play amount with − and +, and both prices follow it. Confirm to start the round. While a stream is on air, the sign and the bar count the live tiles left.
  • The menu button (three lines, at the left of the bar) opens the menu: MANUAL, Turbo and Sound switches, these rules, and your results this session (posts, best win, LIVE streams and your recent posts).
  • MANUAL (in the menu) lets you scratch the tiles yourself: pick any covered tile and drag across it, or tap it to scratch it at once. The space bar scratches a tile for you. The round's result is decided when it starts, so which tile you pick never changes what you win. Autoplay always scratches on its own. A dot on the menu button shows that MANUAL is on.
  • The speaker button turns sound and music on or off. The ⓘ button opens these rules.
  • A big win plays a short celebration; tap or click anywhere on it to skip to the result. Going live (a 3, 2, 1 countdown) and the end of a stream (a recap of the stream) play short scenes too, skipped the same way. During these scenes the bar steps aside and comes back after.
  • The Play button on the opening screen enters the game. If a round was left unfinished (for example after a disconnection), it reads Resume and finishes that round first, at its original play amount.
  • The Hype Meter (the tube beside the board) shows this round's progress toward the LIVE STREAM. It fills with each reaction and Hype Check, empties when a CANCELLED tile ends the round, and bursts into the LIVE STREAM when it is full.
