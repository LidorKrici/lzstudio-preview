The game

Clusters of five or more burst and tumble on a 7x7 temple floor. Stone faces land as 2x2 blocks, eat the wins beside them and erupt into multipliers that double up to x128. Four golden peppers spin the Totem Wheel: twenty prizes up to x5,000, or a night that starts with multipliers on the board.

3 modes with a top win of 10,000x. The math returns 96.30% on every mode and was replayed in full over 192,166 rounds.
