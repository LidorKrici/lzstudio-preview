The game

Pull the band back to HOP, GLIDE or FULL SEND and launch the pilot on her glider over the meadow. Dew and seeds add to the flight, every firefly on the tail doubles what comes after it, and sparrows tear half the haul away. On FULL SEND the frog can end the flight.

5 modes with a top win of 10,000x. The math returns 96.30% on every mode and was replayed in full over 717,023 rounds.
